Conditions

The two condition scalars themselves — overcast and haze — and the drift that moves them live on the Driven tab. This tab is where you author what each condition REACHES.

Cloud AMOUNT follows overcast directly. Everything else it changes — the light, the colours, the water — is held back until there is cloud in the sky to justify it, between these two points.
